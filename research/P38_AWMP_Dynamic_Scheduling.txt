observed arrival history. 2) The afterstate decomposition separates the known, deterministic shop transition from the exogenous
job-arrival process. 3) A pretrained LLM forms the exogenous component of the structured world model and, with online
residual self-calibration, forecasts a compact early-late arrival belief. 4) The belief-conditioned planner first scores feasible
actions, retains the top-K candidates, and evaluates them through short scenario-based rollouts. 5) Meta-evolution optimizes
planning strategies offline. 6) The best strategy is frozen and evaluated on held-out dynamic JSSP and FJSP benchmarks.
