                                        challenge in robotics, in part due to the complexity of planning
                                        and control for contact-rich systems. In reinforcement learn-
                                        ing (RL), one popular approach has been to use massively-
                                        parallelized, domain-randomized simulations to learn a policy                                     Keypoint Predictor
                                        offline over a vast array of contact conditions, allowing robust
                                        sim-to-real transfer. Inspired by recent advances in real-time
